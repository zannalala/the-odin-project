Chicken Soup Recipe

A healthy chicken soup, huge portions and ideal for the holidays.


Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 onions (chopped)
  • 3 medium carrots (chopped)
  • 1 tbsp thyme leaves (roughly chopped)
  • 1.4L chicken stock
  • 300g leftover roast chicken (shredded and skinned)
  • 200g frozen peas
  • 3 tbsp Greek yogurt
  • 1 garlic clove (crushed)
  • Squeeze of lemon juice

Serves 4 people and contains a total of 339kcal per serving.


Steps

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large pan. Add the onions, carrots and thyme leaves, then gently fry for 15mins.
  2. Stir in 1.4L chicken stock, bring to a boil, cover, then simmer for 10mins.
  3. Add the leftover roast chicken, remove half the mixture, then purée with a stick blender. Add frozen peas, seasoning and simmer for 5mins until hot throughout.
  4. Mix in Greek yogurt, garlic and lemon juice. Ladle the soup into bowls, swirl in the yogurt mixture.
  5. Serve and enjoy!
